================================================================================
  URGENT: HOW TO FIX "NOT SECURE" WARNING ON YOUR WEBSITE
================================================================================

The "Not Secure" warning is caused by MISSING SSL/HTTPS CERTIFICATE.

I have removed all dangerous files and added security protections, but you 
MUST install an SSL certificate to remove the browser warning.

================================================================================
  QUICK FIX - Install SSL Certificate
================================================================================

OPTION 1: Free SSL with Let's Encrypt (Recommended)
----------------------------------------------------
1. Log into your hosting control panel (cPanel/Plesk)
2. Look for "SSL/TLS" or "Let's Encrypt" section
3. Click "Install Free SSL Certificate"
4. Select your domain
5. Click "Install"
6. Wait 5-10 minutes for activation

OPTION 2: Through Your Hosting Provider
----------------------------------------
1. Contact your hosting provider support
2. Ask them to install an SSL certificate
3. Most providers offer free SSL certificates

OPTION 3: Cloudflare (Free & Easy)
-----------------------------------
1. Sign up at cloudflare.com
2. Add your website
3. Change your domain's nameservers to Cloudflare's
4. Enable SSL in Cloudflare dashboard (it's free!)
5. Wait 24-48 hours for DNS propagation

================================================================================
  AFTER SSL IS INSTALLED
================================================================================

1. Edit the file: .htaccess (in your website root folder)

2. Find these lines (around line 9-10):
   # RewriteCond %{HTTPS} off
   # RewriteRule ^(.*)$ https://%{HTTP_HOST}%{REQUEST_URI} [L,R=301]

3. Remove the # symbol from both lines to make them active:
   RewriteCond %{HTTPS} off
   RewriteRule ^(.*)$ https://%{HTTP_HOST}%{REQUEST_URI} [L,R=301]

4. Save the file

5. Visit your website - it should now show "Secure" with a padlock icon!
